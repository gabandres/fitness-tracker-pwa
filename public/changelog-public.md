## October 2026

- **Workouts that progress themselves.** The Train tab reads every set you log
  and suggests the next load and reps for each lift, with the reason beside it.
- **Supersets.** Pair two lifts, alternate between them, and see the pairing in
  your report.
- **Fixes.** A logged food keeps its gram weight, every change on Today can be
  undone, and tapping the widget with the app closed no longer crashes it.

## 1.2.5 — October 6, 2026

- Native sheets and menus on iPhone and Android.
- A rest timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island; end a fast from the
  Lock Screen.
- Maintenance calories and trend weight charted over time.
- Larger text sizes lay out properly across the whole app.

## 1.2.4 — September 23, 2026

- A running fast no longer stretches the Dynamic Island over the clock.

## Earlier

Ignia has been free, private and ad-free since day one: calories, protein,
weight, fasting and workouts, with a maintenance estimate measured from your own
weight trend.
