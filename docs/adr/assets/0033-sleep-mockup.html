Why the highlighted bars are the argument. The five blue bars are exactly the nights the sentence is about, so the visual is not decoration next to a claim — it is the claim, drawn. The dashed line is the user's own average, never an 8-hour prescription: Ignia has no authority to tell anyone how long to sleep. The gap on night 9 is a missing night; it is drawn as a hairline, never as a zero and never interpolated. The big number is a duration, never a score.

The two frames above are the two different no-data truths, and the difference is the point (ADR-0026's empty-state rule). Left: nothing is connected — the row is an invitation. Right: a source IS connected and has produced nothing, which is a fact about the integration, not about the user's sleep, and Ignia must say so rather than showing an empty chart. Neither is a card, neither has a section header, and both are one line tall — a permanently-empty widget on a screen this dense is the “generic dashboard” failure.
